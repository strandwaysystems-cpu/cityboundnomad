Cookie Policy

Last updated: 2026-09-06

The short version

Nothing non-essential loads until you choose. If you decline, or never answer the banner, no analytics or marketing scripts run at all. The consent manager is fail-closed, meaning the absence of a decision is treated as a refusal.

Categories

  • Strictly necessary: a single local storage entry recording your consent choice so the banner does not reappear on every page. Always on; it is what makes the rest of this policy enforceable.
  • Analytics: off by default. When enabled, aggregate analytics measure which pages are read and which cities people care about. IP addresses are anonymised and Google Signals is disabled, so no cross-device advertising profile is built.
  • Affiliate: off by default. When enabled, affiliate attribution may set cookies. These are set by the partner, not by this site, and only after you click through to them.

Changing your mind

Clear this site's cookies and local storage in your browser settings and the consent banner returns on the next page load, letting you make a different choice.
